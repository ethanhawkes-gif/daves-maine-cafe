Hi, I'm Dave.

Eleven seasons in, and we opened as a beer café — a cozy spot near the Kittery outlets with rotating taps and a bourbon list I'm genuinely proud of. Then folks tried the lobster rolls, and word got around. Today we're Dave's Maine Cafe, and that lobster-shaped flight board on the bar says it all: a real Maine lobster shack with a beer-café heart.

It's still my family running the place. I cook the food I'd want to eat — honest, generous, unmistakably Maine — and there's no hurry to push you out the door. Board games on the shelf, room for the whole family, the game on if there's a game on.
