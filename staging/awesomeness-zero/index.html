Hi. I'm Dave.

I was born and raised in Maine, for better or worse. After 25 years in the outdoor business, I left corporate life to spend more time with Catherine and our kids. Naturally, I opened a beer cafe—which is not how most people create free time.

Maine Beer Cafe started with craft beer, grew a menu larger than our kitchen had any business handling, and eventually got hijacked by a lobster roll. I told the whole sideways story on Facebook—the good days, the sold-out days, and the ideas that probably should have stayed in my head. Somewhere in there, Maine Beer Cafe became Dave's Maine Cafe.

Now that lobster roll has led us to Route One Bottling Co., where we're taking the same Route 1 point of view into craft sodas, hot sauces, and BBQ sauces. Apparently running a tiny cafe wasn't enough. I also picked up a pickleball addiction, because one questionable life choice at a time felt too organized.
