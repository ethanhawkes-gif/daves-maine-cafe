Hi. I'm Dave.

I left the corporate world because I wanted more time with Catherine and our kids. Then I opened a little beer cafe because I loved craft beer.

The menu got bigger. The kitchen did not. Then the lobster roll became the thing people told their friends about, which around here counts as a marketing department.

I run this place the same way I show it on Facebook: in real time. What's fresh. What sold out. What went sideways. What I'm trying next. This isn't a chain. Most days, I'm the guy making your lunch.
