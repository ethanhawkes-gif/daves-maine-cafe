The origin story

Before soda got boring.

Glass Only. Always.

There was a pop shop Dave used to stop at as a kid — the kind of place where the refrigerator cases were full of glass bottles in flavors you didn't know you needed until you saw them. Root beer that actually tasted like root beer. Cream soda that was worth a small argument. A lime rickey that required no explanation beyond being cold and good.

Nobody asked if it was artisanal. Nobody put it in a can with a lifestyle on the label. You just picked one, paid whatever it cost, and felt unreasonably good about the whole thing.

That place is gone. Most of them are. But the feeling doesn't have to be. Route One Bottling Co. is Dave's version of it — glass bottles because plastic is a cop-out, flavors because they're the whole point, and a lobster on the label because this is Maine and someone had to.

For the grown-ups

The soda got a promotion.

A few of the flavors have been quietly moonlighting in the cocktail program. Dave's aware. Dave approved them.

Cocktail 01

The Yard Sale

Bourbon · Route 01 Root Beer · A Regret

A proper bourbon float in a cold glass bottle. Dave had a theory that root beer and good bourbon were basically the same food group. Three years of research confirms he was right. Garnish: the bourbon list is 70 bottles long — pick carefully.

Vodka · Route 02 Ginger Beer · Lime · Regionally Earned Confidence

Maine's answer to a Moscow Mule — except we didn't invent it, we just made it better by doing it in a roadside cafe 400 feet from the ocean. Copper mug optional. Glass bottle required. Crustacean Nation approved.

Cocktail 03

The Dark & Rickey

Dark Rum · Route 06 Raspberry Lime Rickey · A General Optimism

The Rickey was already doing most of the work. Dark rum just showed up and took credit, which honestly is very on-brand for dark rum. Tart, fruity, cold, and more drinkable than it has any right to be. Proceed at your own pace.

Cocktail 04

The Sunburn

Tequila · Route 05 Orange · Lime · Zero Regrets (Yet)

Maine doesn't really do sunburns until the first genuinely warm day of the year — then everyone gets one simultaneously and acts surprised. This is that drink. Tequila, Route 05 orange, lime, ice cold. Whether you call it a Tequila Sunrise or a Route 1 problem, the orange is doing the work and it knows it.
