Before soda got boring.

Dave remembers when the flavors were the whole point. Root beer tasted like root beer. Cream soda was worth arguing over. Lime Rickey did not need a marketing department. Route One Bottling Co. is bringing that feeling back—one glass bottle, one roadside stop, and one mildly unsupervised lobster at a time.
