The roll that built the place

Hi, I'm Dave.
The roll changed the plan.

I left the corporate world because I wanted more time with Catherine and our kids. Then I opened a little beer café because I loved craft beer.

The menu got bigger, the kitchen did not, and somewhere along the way the lobster roll became the thing people told their friends about. Around here, that counts as a marketing department.

I run the café the same way I show it on Facebook: in real time. What's fresh, what sold out, what went sideways, and what I'm trying next. This isn't a chain. Most days, I'm the guy making your lunch.

The Weekly Lineup

Five days open, a different reason to pull over on each one.

Wednesday · 11:30 AM–7 PM

Shell Yeah Wednesday

The seafood happy hour that refuses to die, and we're glad about it. Stuffed clams, Jonah crab claws, soup and shellfish specials — ask what's shucked today.

Thursday · 11:30 AM–7 PM

Dave's Lobster Roll Day

$18 all day. The roll that made us, at the price that makes our bookkeeper itch. Fresh supply only — first roll sets the bar, last roll wins the trip.

Friday · 11:30 AM–7 PM

Bourbon Friday

$9 pours, all day, off one of the Seacoast's deepest bourbon lists. 70+ bottles. No stunt, no gimmick — just the good shelf at a price our accountant describes as "concerning."

Saturday & Sunday · 11:30 AM–5 PM

Weekend on Route 1

Full menu, full bar, and the last good lunch before the bridge. Outlet shoppers, leaf peepers and locals who know better — all welcome.
