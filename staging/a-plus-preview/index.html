The person to blame

Hi. I'm Dave.

I left the corporate world because I wanted more time with Catherine and our kids. Then I opened a little beer cafe on Route 1 because I loved craft beer.

The menu got bigger. The kitchen did not. Then the lobster roll became the thing people told their friends about, which around here counts as a marketing department.

I run this place the same way I show it on Facebook: in real time. What's fresh. What sold out. What went sideways. What I'm trying next. This isn't a chain. Most days, I'm the guy making your lunch.

Route 1 roadside intelligence

Answers before hunger decides.

The things people ask on the sidewalk, in the car, and at the outlets.

Where can I get a lobster roll near the Kittery Outlets?

Dave's Maine Cafe is at 439 US Route 1, Ste 1 in Kittery, Maine, on the same stretch of Route 1 as the Kittery Outlets and minutes from Portsmouth, NH.

When are Dave's lobster rolls $18?

Every Thursday while the daily supply lasts. The same fresh-picked Maine lobster roll is $20 on the other open days.

What are Dave's Maine Cafe's hours?

Wednesday–Friday 11:30 AM–7 PM. Saturday–Sunday 11:30 AM–5 PM. Closed Monday and Tuesday. Holiday changes get posted on Facebook first.

Can I order a lobster roll online for pickup?

Yes. Order pickup on Toast, or call (207) 475-5655 for the current lobster situation.

Does Dave's have a bar?

16 rotating Maine-leaning taps, 70+ bourbons and whiskeys with $9 pours every Friday, shot-and-beer combos, Maine THC beverages and wine by the glass. See the bar list.

Do you do catering or big orders?

Yes: lobster rolls and seafood for a crowd, party platters and comfort-food spreads. Catering & large orders in Kittery.
