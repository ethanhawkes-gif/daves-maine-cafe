Useful questions

Before you ask Dave anyway

How much notice do you need?

More is better, especially for large lobster-roll counts and holiday dates. Send the request even if the clock is already suspiciously short.

Is delivery available?

Scheduled pickup at the cafe is standard. Tell us where your event is and Dave will confirm any available logistics with the quote.

Can the group order different items?

Yes. Tell us the mix you have in mind and we'll keep the plan as organized as a tiny Route 1 cafe reasonably can.

Is submitting the form a reservation?

No. Your order is booked only after Dave confirms the menu, timing, availability, and payment details.
