# Dave's Maine Café — Brand Kit

The single source of truth for the brand. Website, menu, merch, email, and signage all pull from this so everything looks like one thing. Transcribed/decided with Dave 2026-07-14→15.

> **Rule:** Concepts and copy get locked in TEXT here first; visual proofs come after. (Dave's efficiency rule.)

---

## 1. Colors — "Red, White & Blue, Maine-style"
Navy + red + cream/gold reads as patriotic RWB, which is intentional (Lobsterpalooza, summer tourist peak, America's 250th). Hex values are the live site CSS.

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary dark / grounds, headers | Navy | `#182b33` (deep `#203b45`) |
| Accent / lobster / "hot" | Lobster Red | `#b83a2b` (deep `#9c2f22`) |
| Light / paper / shirt base | Cream | `#f7efdd` · Paper `#f2e7cf` |
| Highlight / gold pop | Gold | `#d79a37` (deep `#c2872a`) |
| Text | Ink | `#241d15` (soft `#4a3f30`) |
| Secondary (sparingly) | Forest Green | `#2f5b3f` |

Avoid putting the red lobster on a red garment (it disappears). On dark/navy, flip banner text to cream.

## 2. Fonts — the 4-font system
| Job | Font | Where |
|-----|------|-------|
| LOUD (brand name, headlines, signage) | **Anton** | "DAVE'S MAINE CAFÉ", section titles |
| LABEL (kickers, buttons, EXIT 123, badge ring) | **Oswald** | small structural caps |
| CHARACTER (taglines, roadside asides) | **Special Elite** (typewriter) | personality lines — use sparingly; this is the retro secret weapon |
| BODY (menu text, paragraphs) | **Inter** | readable body copy |

All free (Google Fonts). **LOCKED (4 core fonts).** PENDING only: whether merch/badge uses a chunkier vintage slab for the LOUD role instead of Anton.*

## 5. Voice & tone
Warm, funny, local, Route 1 roadside wit. A trusted café regular who's great at the back office. Never corporate, never slop. "If you know, you know" insider energy for locals; curiosity for tourists.

**Positioning vibe (Dave's words, 2026-07-15):** road-trip · lobster shack · Route 1 *destination*. The place you pull over for — a stop worth the drive, not a roadside afterthought. Everything should feel like a classic New England shack you'd detour for.

## 6. Standing brand rules
- **10 Years Strong · EST. 2016** (consistent everywhere; not 2015).
- **$20** is the public face price for the lobster roll everywhere.
- **Thursday = "Dave's Lobster Roll Day"** — a celebration; the $18 is real in-store but stays OFF public web/marketing copy (mystery for locals).
- **Tourist's Revenge** — possessive spelling, everywhere.
- Weekly rhythm is a brand asset: Dave's Day (Tue) · Shell Yeah Wednesday · Dave's Lobster Roll Day (Thu) · Bourbon & Bugs Friday · Tourist Revenge Weekend.

## 7. Where it applies
Website (davesmainecafe.com), printed menu, specials, email signature, signage. **Merch/apparel is its own brand — Revolver under Double D Studios (Dylan + Dave)** — it uses this visual language but is labeled as the studio line, not as Dave's Maine Café.
