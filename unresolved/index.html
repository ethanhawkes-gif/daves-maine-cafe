05 / THE FIRST 30 DAYS

Start small.
Stay recognizable.

WEEK 1

Establish the feeling

Post four philosophy films. No selling. Make the palette, pace, and typography consistent.

WEEK 2

Open the conversation

Ask one real question. Reply like a person. Save audience language that feels true to the Lexicon.

WEEK 3

Show the making

Bring Dave + Dylan into frame through hands, notes, voice, debate, and process—not a founder commercial.

WEEK 4

Reveal one object

Let UNCERTAINTY become the first artifact. Invite interest; do not manufacture urgency.
